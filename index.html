Common questions

A purchase didn't arrive.
Open Market or Settings and tap Restore Purchases. If it still doesn't show up, email me with the date and the item. Refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?
Create an account in Settings › Account on your old phone, then sign in with it on the new one. Your progress is backed up to the cloud.

How do I delete my account?
Settings › Delete account deletes your account and its cloud data immediately. See the privacy policy for details.

Can I turn off ads or tracking?
Ads only play when you choose to watch one for a reward. You can change ad consent in Settings › Privacy › Privacy options and turn off usage stats in Settings › Privacy.

Accessibility
The game supports VoiceOver, larger text, a color-blind palette, reduced motion, and haptics and sound toggles in Settings.

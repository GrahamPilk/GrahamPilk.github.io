Privacy Policy

Mutagen: Rooted (iOS) · Last updated November 1, 2026

Mutagen: Rooted ("the game") is made by Graham Pilkington ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the game collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The short version: you can play the whole game without an account, ads are optional, and I never sell your personal data.

What the game collects

1. Your progress, on your phone

Your save (organisms, floors, currencies, settings) is stored on your device. It never leaves your phone unless you create an account (below).

2. An optional account (Supabase)

If you choose to sign up, I store your email address, a password (hashed, never readable), the display name you pick, and your cloud save and scores. This is used only to back up your progress and sync it between devices. The account service is Supabase, which stores this data on servers in the United States.

3. Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. I never see your card or payment details. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history so the game can deliver and restore what you bought.

4. Optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

The game only shows ads you choose to watch for a reward, and they only unlock later in the game. When you watch one, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when iOS asks), ad interactions, coarse location derived from your IP address, and app performance and crash data, to show and measure ads. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices first. You can change them any time in Settings › Privacy › Privacy options.
  • If you tell iOS not to allow tracking, ads are less personalized. The game and its rewards stay exactly the same.

5. Anonymous usage stats (PostHog)

To learn which parts of the game work and which don't (for example, where players quit the tutorial), the game sends anonymous gameplay events such as "floor started", "floor won" or "tutorial step reached" to PostHog. These events use a random ID created on your phone. They never include your name, email, account, advertising ID or location, and IP addresses are discarded. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage stats.

Deleting your data

  • Account: open Settings › Delete account in the game. This permanently deletes your account, email, display name, cloud save and scores right away.
  • Local data: deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone.
  • You can also email me (below) to ask for a copy of your data or for its deletion.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email me and I will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to or limit its use. Email me to make any of these requests.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update it here and change the date at the top.
